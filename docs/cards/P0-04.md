the observed executable-link `EACCES`; another error, a readable inode mismatch
or loss of a previously successful check fails closed. QMP still requires exact
`SO_PEERCRED`, signals use `pidfd_send_signal`, and private leaves remain until
pidfd readiness and the direct `Popen` return code both prove exit.

Under persistent executable-link denial, a same-task re-exec with identical
argv cannot be distinguished without extra kernel or child cooperation. The
authenticated QEMU binary in the read-only controller image remains trusted;
cmdline is corroboration, not executable identity. The unprivileged,
capability-free, offline and zero-swap container is unchanged, and no ptrace
