# P0-06 — HIGHLY EXPERIMENTAL interactive VM handoff

**Status:** Ready for the manual experimental appliance workflow; OS qualification remains open

**Owner:** `card/P0-06-interactive-vm`  
**Invariants:** INV-12 (source and artifact binding only)

Build a manual, offline development-appliance producer from the already retained
generic OCI. The producer must keep the production image immutable, modify only a
new appliance disk, use a locked credential-free local fixture account, validate the
first graphical login, remove provisioning material, retain an exact compressed
qcow2 for two days, and independently reboot those retained bytes read-only.

This card creates no release, signing, security, containment, update, or rollback
claim. The qcow2 is **HIGHLY EXPERIMENTAL** and must not hold real data or accounts.
The first slice has no NIC.

## Acceptance

- Manual workflow only; exact current repository commit/tree and reviewed generic
  OCI are bound before installation. Preflight run `46125049529` selects the current
  combined generic/NVIDIA lock and exact integrated verifier.
- The installer selects `graphical.target`; both boots expose virtio display,
  keyboard, and pointer devices, use TCG multi-thread execution with an explicit
  two-CPU, one-socket, two-core topology, and disable networking.
- The appliance-only account is locked, outside `wheel`, and has no password or key.
  No account or fixture byte enters the production OCI.
- SDDM, `symbi.desktop`, Hyprland, Waybar, the portal payload, and all seeded
  defaults are observed in the installed guest. Walker is launched in a bounded
  transient user unit, observed by its executable, stopped successfully, and then
  observed absent before acceptance.
- Provisioning units, scripts, credentials, and boot wants do not remain on disk.
- A compressed qcow2 is hash-bound in `manifest.json`, expires after two days, and
  remains byte-identical across an independent read-only-backing reboot that emits a
  nonblank source-bound screenshot.
- The seven-day evidence artifact retains the exact source-bound reboot result,
  manifest, screenshot, controller record, bounded logs, and outer systemd record
  within a fixed 32 MiB aggregate ceiling. The screenshot remains individually
  limited to 4 MiB; no extra file is accepted.
- The outer producer uses a named asynchronous `Type=oneshot` unit retained after
  exit. Its numeric terminal status, 5 GiB ceiling, zero-swap/OOM facts, and usage
  are captured while loaded and synced before stop/reset. Upload authorization is
  emitted only after reset reports `LoadState=not-found`, the captured control-group
  path is gone, and the final evidence inventory and contents pass validation. A
  collection, cleanup, swap, OOM, or final-validation failure removes the evidence
  and public output and cannot authorize upload.
- The public download is separately restricted to the qcow2, manifest, and screenshot;
  all three are rehashed, credential-scanned, and cross-bound immediately before
  upload. The manifest expiry is bound to the hosted workflow clock.
- A controller failure before evidence upload leaves every boot, graphical, artifact,
  independent-reboot, OS, release, and signature claim false and removes the user
  artifact. Its failure artifact exists only when the wrapper successfully sanitizes
  and validates the exact controller and systemd records. The controller record may
  embed exact resource facts, a digest-bound child failure, and bounded redacted tails
  from only the approved QEMU and serial logs; linked, special, unsafe, and oversized
  inputs are rejected. Earlier preparation
  failures and failed sanitization or validation retain no appliance evidence
  artifact. A later public revalidation mismatch blocks the qcow2 upload; already
  uploaded diagnostic evidence may remain and records only the earlier controller
  result.
- The producer and independent reboot containers are each fixed at 4 GiB, zero swap,
  two CPUs, and 64 tasks inside the exact 5 GiB outer unit, leaving 1 GiB for its
  controller and container monitor.

The producer and offline contract tests are preparation. Hosted runtime evidence is
still required before this card can report a downloadable, bootable result. The
controller requires at least 60 GiB of free hosted-runner disk after preparation.
The generic virtio/TCG
gate does not use or qualify physical NVIDIA hardware.

The workflow validates the retained preflight with the controller's validator before
the 4,177,421,526-byte download. Its capacity floor adds those exact bytes and a
1 GiB preparation margin to the controller's 60 GiB minimum. Step limits total 228
minutes (5 + 1 + 2 + 20 + 20 + 130 + 10 + 10 + 30) inside a 240-minute job, leaving 12
minutes of outer margin. The 20-minute download contains sequential 30-second
metadata and 900-second archive caps, leaving 4.5 minutes for hashing and wrapper
work. The controller's 115-minute unit therefore has 15 minutes inside its step for
cleanup and evidence before the bounded evidence upload, public revalidation, and
download upload.
